About Uttarakhand Radio

Uttarakhand Radio (Dev Bhoomi FM) is a personal, non-commercial web radio that streams folk music from the Indian Himalayan state of Uttarakhand — Garhwali, Kumaoni and wider Pahadi traditions.

All recordings are sourced from the public Internet Archive. Credit belongs to the original performers, researchers and institutions (IGRMS, CCRT, ICCR and independent uploaders).

If you are an artist or rights-holder and would like a recording removed, please open an issue on the GitHub repo.
